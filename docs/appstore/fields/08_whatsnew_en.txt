Moa+ 2.1.1 makes haptic feedback feel immediate.

[Improved]
- Haptic feedback now fires the moment you press a key, not when you lift your finger. Moa+ is a swipe keyboard, so your finger stays down while you draw a stroke; buzzing only at the end felt late. It now responds on touch down, like the system keyboard. (Reported in GitHub issue #23)

[From 2.1: text shortcuts]
- Shortcuts can include punctuation such as periods and question marks, at the beginning or at the end.
- A shortcut is recognized even when you type it directly after a word, with no space in between.
- You can choose whether the space that confirms a shortcut stays after the replacement.
- Undo on backspace right after a replacement can be turned on or off in settings.
- Very short shortcuts trigger by accident easily, so new ones are limited by default. You can lift the limit in settings, and shortcuts you already saved keep working.
- Fixed the keyboard being cut off while the suggestion bar was showing, and typing not responding after returning from another app.

[From 2.0: input overhaul]
Typing behavior was matched to the original MoaKey through frame-by-frame analysis of real device recordings.
- Diagonal out-and-back strokes for ae, e and ui.
- Vertical round-trip chains (wa, wae, yeo, ye, yu and more).
- Dot key combinations, including the o/yo and u/yu toggle.
- Backspace deletes one jamo at a time: final consonant, then vowel, then consonant.

Core typing works without Full Access. Haptic feedback requires iOS Full Access (optional); your input is never transmitted off your device even when it is enabled.
